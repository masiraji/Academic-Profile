Meet the team Join the lab
Our Vision

A future in which the light we live under, the climate we live in, and the selves we bring to both are understood rigorously, measured fairly, and studied for every population, not only a few.

Our Mission

The Lumen and Self Lab studies how the human environment, light and climate, interacts with the human self, personality and psychological functioning, and builds the culturally valid measurement tools needed to study both. Working from Bangladesh with partners worldwide, we pursue open and reproducible science, generate evidence that can inform health, design and policy in the Global South, and train the next generation of psychological scientists to do the same.

Why “Lumen and Self”

Lumen names the environment that acts on us: light first, and by extension the climate we inhabit. Self names the person who receives it: their personality, cognition and wellbeing. Psychometrics is the bridge that lets us measure both faithfully.

What the mission commits us to

Discover how light and climate shape alertness, cognition, sleep, wellbeing and behaviour in everyday life.

Measure well by developing, adapting and validating instruments that work in Bangla and across cultures.

Understand the person by studying personality and the self as the lens through which environments are experienced.

Share openly through preregistration, open data, open code and open instruments.

Translate findings for lighting practice, climate adaptation, education and public health.

Train students and early-career researchers in rigorous, reproducible methods.
